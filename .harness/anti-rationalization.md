# Anti-Rationalization

- Do not skip the harness integrity check at session start.
- Do not commit completed work without a changelog entry.
- Do not treat private harness files as public sync artifacts.
- Do not implement trading or knowledge-base features inside SIGMA.
- Do not leave failed verification unrecorded in `.harness/progress.md`.
- Do not use lint-disable comments to suppress violations instead of fixing them.
- Do not keep harness rules that have never been triggered in practice.
- Do not treat harness file count or `progress.md` length as a measure of quality.
